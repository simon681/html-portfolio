About Me

I’m Murshidul Hasan, a passionate marketing strategist and digital media leader with over 14 years of experience in transforming brands through innovation, insight, and integrated communications. My career has taken me through diverse industries—ranging from e-commerce and banking to media agencies—where I’ve helped top brands like Banglalink, Marico, ACI, and BSRM navigate the evolving digital landscape.

Currently serving as an Assistant Director at GroupM Bangladesh, I specialize in media buying, performance marketing, programmatic advertising, and data-driven brand growth. I bring hands-on expertise in platforms like Google Ads, Meta Ads, DV360, and have certifications in analytics, digital strategy, and marketing technologies. My academic foundation includes an MBA in Marketing from IBA, University of Dhaka, and a BBA in Finance & Economics from North South University.

Now venturing into a new chapter, I’m upskilling in Business Analytics and Data Science to future-proof my career and deepen my impact. Whether it's building high-performance campaigns, advising on digital transformations, or exploring entrepreneurial paths in advertising and technology, I’m always looking to combine creativity with measurable impact. This site is my space to share what I’ve learned, what I’m building, and where I’m headed next.
