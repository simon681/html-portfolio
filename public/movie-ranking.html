My top 3 movies of all time.

The Dark Knight

A masterpiece of storytelling and character development. Heath Ledger’s Joker redefined the superhero genre.

Inception

A mind-bending experience. The concept, music, and visuals are unforgettable.

Forrest Gump

An emotional journey that blends history, heart, and humor—truly timeless.
